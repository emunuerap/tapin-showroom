# TapIn Products Universe · Marketing Brief

## Purpose

`/products` must be the clearest and strongest marketing journey in the TapIn showroom.
It should not repeat the Guests/Venues story. Guests/Venues explains who TapIn serves.
Products explains what TapIn is made of, how the pieces connect, and why that connected system is a major advantage for restaurants, hospitality groups, and guests.

The page should feel like entering TapIn from the inside: one protocol moving through every surface.

## Core Message

TapIn is not a booking widget, not an app, and not a chatbot.
TapIn is a connected hospitality protocol:

- It captures intent where demand already exists.
- It verifies guests with less friction.
- It remembers taste, behavior, pace, privacy, and gratitude.
- It protects revenue through payments, no-show logic, and smarter routing.
- It gives venues an operational layer before service begins.
- It connects web, app, messaging, POS, payments, NFC, suppliers, and back office.

The marketing promise:

> Choose TapIn and every surface of hospitality starts working together.

## What Products Must Not Do

- Do not repeat the desktop/mobile Guests/Venues chapter structure.
- Do not present features as disconnected cards.
- Do not rely on generic SaaS dashboards.
- Do not overuse decorative abstract effects without explaining product value.
- Do not introduce amber as a product color in this section. Products uses Obsidian, Silver, and Yuzu.
- Do not make each product feel like a separate mini-startup. Everything must feed back into TapIn Core.

## Narrative Arc

### 1. Product Core

Show TapIn as a living system.
The opening must communicate:

- One core intelligence.
- Six surfaces orbiting it.
- Every node sends and receives signal.
- The product is larger than the sum of its features.

Visual direction:
Obsidian depth, yuzu signal rings, silver interface lines, premium floating nodes, no flat SaaS cards.

### 2. Web SDK

Message:
The restaurant already has traffic. TapIn turns that traffic into verified, memory-aware, payment-ready guests.

Show:

- A realistic restaurant website.
- TapIn floating entrypoint.
- A conversion path: visitor intent -> guest memory -> protected action.
- Bottom sheet reservation flow.
- Confirmation feels fast, trusted, and native.

Benefit:
More bookings, less friction, better guest context, stronger no-show protection.

### 3. Consumer App

Message:
The app is not another reservation app. It is the guest passport.

Show:

- Taste Genome.
- Reservation wallet.
- Visit flashbacks.
- Gratitude memory.
- Personal preference signals becoming useful hospitality context.

Benefit:
Guests feel known without repeating themselves. Venues receive context before service.

### 4. Hospitality OS

Message:
The venue gets operational intelligence before the shift starts.

Show:

- Floor state.
- Guest routing.
- No-show risk.
- Table yield.
- Tetris Agent recommendations.

Benefit:
The room moves with intelligence, not guesswork.

### 5. Messaging Layer

Message:
Reservations happen where conversations already happen.

Show:

- WhatsApp and Telegram intent.
- TapIn parsing natural language.
- Availability returned.
- Confirmation link.
- Venue context updated automatically.

Benefit:
Demand is captured without forcing users into a new channel.

### 6. AI Core

Message:
TapIn AI is operational intelligence, not chatbot noise.

Show:

- Command parsing.
- Taste Genome.
- Sentiment Matrix.
- Tetris Agent.
- Gratitude Protocol.
- Decision path: parse -> decide -> act.

Benefit:
Signals become actions that improve service, yield, memory, and trust.

### 7. Integrations & Payments

Message:
TapIn connects the room, the guest, and the outside world.

Show:

- POS.
- Stripe.
- NFC.
- Website.
- WhatsApp/Telegram.
- Maps/social.
- Supplier invoices.
- Back-office memory.

Benefit:
Restaurants reduce fragmentation. Data stops living in separate tools and starts improving the guest journey.

## Visual Language

Palette:

- Obsidian: core background and depth.
- Silver: text, structure, quiet UI.
- Yuzu: intelligence, action, confirmation, signal.

Avoid:

- Amber in Products.
- Purple/blue gradients.
- Generic neon cyberpunk.
- Empty black cards.
- Decorative cards that do not explain product mechanics.

Motion:

- Text reveals should feel editorial and intentional.
- Product mechanics should animate as cause and effect.
- Scroll should reveal connection, not just content.
- Every major section should answer: what signal enters, what TapIn understands, what action happens, why it matters.

## Asset Plan

Highest-priority generated assets:

1. Product Core cinematic loop.
2. Realistic restaurant website atmosphere for Web SDK.
3. Premium iPhone passport render for Consumer App.
4. AI Core signal engine loop.
5. Integration fabric/back-office environment.

Generated assets should be atmospheric layers, not the interface itself. Real labels, buttons, and interactive states stay in React so the page remains sharp, accessible, and alive.
